# JARVIS 1.3.1 — Male voice fix

Installs over 1.0–1.3 (same application ID and signing key; data is kept).

* **Voice** – JARVIS now binds to Google Speech Services when installed, so its English (UK) male voices appear in
  Settings → Voice. New **Male voices only** switch (default on) never auto-picks or lists known female voices; voices
  are marked ♂ / ♀ in the list.
* **Cleaner JARVIS voice** – removed the short "digital double" echo that made the voice sound doubled.

Hologram workspace, hand tracking, wake-word modes and screen-off wake come from 1.3 (see that release).
